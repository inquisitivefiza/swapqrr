1. Introduction

SwapQR , developed by LeafNode Labs, helps you share professional contact details — such as your name, phone number, LinkedIn profile, and GitHub profile — as a scannable QR code, and to scan and save others' details in return. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app handles and how.

2. Information You Provide

SwapQR stores information that you choose to enter to create your QR business card:

  • Name (required)
  • Phone number (required)
  • Email address (optional)
  • Occupation (optional)
  • LinkedIn profile URL (optional)
  • GitHub profile URL (optional)

Name and phone number are required to create your card. All other fields are optional and can be left blank. This information is stored only on your device and is never sent to us.

3. App Permissions

SwapQR requests the following device permission:

  • Camera — used to scan QR codes shared by others. Camera frames are processed on-device in real time and are not recorded or transmitted anywhere.

SwapQR does not request access to your Contacts, Location, Storage/Photos, or Microphone.

4. How Your Information Is Used

The information you enter is used solely to:

  • Generate a QR code representing your details, for you to share with others
  • Store details you scan from other people's QR codes, so you can find them again later within the app

We do not use this information for advertising, analytics, or any purpose beyond providing this core functionality on your device.

5. QR Code Sharing — Important Note

Once you generate and share a QR code, anyone who scans it can see the information encoded in it — including with their phone's standard camera app, without needing SwapQR installed. This is intentional (it's what makes the app useful for quickly sharing contact info at interviews, hackathons, and conferences), but it also means you should only include information in your QR code that you're comfortable sharing with whoever scans it.

We have no ability to control, track, or revoke a QR code once you've shared it, printed it, or displayed it to someone, since it functions as a standard, offline-readable code (similar to a vCard/business card) rather than a link back to our systems.

6. Data Storage & Retention

All information — both what you enter about yourself and what you scan from others — is stored locally on your device only, using standard Android storage components (a local Room database and DataStore preferences). We do not operate any server, and none of this information is transmitted to us or stored anywhere outside your device.

This data remains on your device until you delete it within the app or uninstall the app, at which point it is permanently removed.

7. Third-Party Services

SwapQR uses Google ML Kit (delivered through Google Play Services) for on-device QR code scanning. Scanning happens locally on your device, and we do not receive any data from this process. Google Play Services may download the scanning model the first time it is used, and Google may collect technical or diagnostic data in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. QR codes are generated on your device using the open-source ZXing library, without any network access.

We do not use advertising SDKs, analytics platforms, or crash-reporting tools.

8. Security

Because your data stays on your device rather than on a server we control, its security depends on your device's own protections (screen lock, OS-level encryption, and keeping your device's software updated). We recommend using a secure lock screen, especially since your app data includes personal contact details.

9. Your Rights & Choices

You can view, edit, or delete any information you've entered or scanned at any time directly within the app. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all locally stored data. Because we hold no copy of your data on any server, there is nothing further for us to delete on your behalf.

10. Children's Privacy

SwapQR is intended for professional networking use (e.g., interviews, hackathons, conferences) and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the app's features evolve. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.
